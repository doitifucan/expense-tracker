Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including your name, email address, and the financial transaction data (amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions) that you enter into the app. We also collect basic usage data to help improve the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide and maintain the expense tracking service. We use your transaction data to generate summaries, reports, and insights tailored to your spending habits. We do not sell your personal or financial data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

We store your data using secure, industry-standard encryption. Data is stored on secure servers with restricted access. You can request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

We may use third-party infrastructure providers (such as cloud hosting services) to store and serve our application. These providers are bound by strict confidentiality agreements and are prohibited from using your data for any purpose other than providing the service to us.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at privacy@spendly.example.com.
